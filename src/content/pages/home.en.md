productBenefitsTitle: Operational value
productBenefits:
  - Sales and contracts — accurate count and size data reduce mismatches with buyers.
  - Harvest and margin — early, precise size distribution improves planning and lowers commercial risk.
  - Escapes and control — periodic accurate counts can reveal small losses; in an incident, data supports an unambiguous tally of fish lost.
  - Sea lice and delousing — a population-level view supports tuning treatment workflows and welfare, unlike very small samples from manual or cage-based methods.
  - Skin health and pricing — automated assessment of wounds and scale loss supports welfare during treatment, price negotiations, and earlier harvest when risk rises.
  - Sexual maturation (in development) — whole-population registration informs interventions, harvest timing, and contract risk better than spot samples alone.

faqTitle: Questions & answers
faqLead: Common questions — organised by topic.
faqTabs:
  - label: Product & use
    items:
      - question: What is Salmoscan?
        answer: |
          Salmoscan is a tool for fish welfare and process control on treatment vessels. It analyses high-quality images and provides lice counts, scale loss and wound assessment, with operational decision support and data for stock analysis.
      - question: Where is the equipment installed?
        answer: |
          Sensors are mounted in pipe flow in the transfer line, typically after dewatering. The system does not measure in the well; it can be placed before and after treatment steps to track welfare and effect.
  - label: Technical
    items:
      - question: What flow speeds does it support?
        answer: |
          The solution is designed for fish passing the camera at high speed (typically up to 5 m/s), with high-resolution machine vision, tailored lighting, and AI-based analysis.
      - question: What about resolution and power use?
        answer: |
          Around 0.1 mm per pixel is stated as the target detail level. Power use is on the order of 100 W plus 8 bar air — see the product section for up-to-date figures.
