## 3. What we process and why

### 3.1 Contact form and e-mail

If you use the contact form or write to us by e-mail, we process:

- name

**Purpose:** to respond to your enquiry and route it inside the company.  
**Legal basis:** GDPR Art. 6(1)(f) legitimate interest in handling business enquiries; or Art. 6(1)(b) where the enquiry is a step toward a contract.  
**Retention:** *[to be completed — e.g. enquiries are kept in e-mail for up to 24 months, then deleted unless a customer relationship or legal obligation requires longer retention]*.  
**Recipients:** messages are delivered by e-mail via our contact API / SMTP provider to company mailboxes. *[Name SMTP / hosting provider if applicable]*.

### 3.2 Website technical logs

Our hosting environment may automatically process IP address, user agent, request URL/time, and similar server-log data for security, troubleshooting, and abuse prevention.

**Legal basis:** Art. 6(1)(f) legitimate interest in secure operation.  
**Retention:** *[to be completed — typical 30–90 days unless needed longer for security incidents]*.

### 3.3 Theme preference (not a cookie)

The site stores your light/dark theme choice in the browser’s **localStorage** (`theme`). This stays on your device and is not sent to our servers as part of ordinary page views. See also our [Cookie policy](/cookies/).

### 3.4 Fonts and third-party resources

The site currently loads fonts from Google Fonts (`fonts.googleapis.com` / `fonts.gstatic.com`). Your browser may then contact Google’s servers (IP address and request data). We do not use Google Analytics or advertising pixels on this site at the time of this draft.

**Legal basis / note:** *[Recommend self-hosting fonts or documenting transfer/consent approach before go-live.]*

### 3.5 What we do not do (current site)
